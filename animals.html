Ocean Animals

Many amazing animals live in the ocean, read on to learn more about them!

Sharks

Image Citation: Unsplash

Sharks are notorious ocean predators. Sharks are a special because their body is made out of cartilage instead of bones like other fish. They use their sharp teeth and strong senses to hunt for food. Sharks vary in size and there are many differnt species.

Octopuses

Image Citation: Unsplash

Octopuses are soft-bodied animals with eight arms. They can change color to hide from predators and are known for being very smart problem-solvers. They also have no bones!

Whales

Image Citation: Unsplash

Whales are some of the largest animals on Earth. They are mammals and they breathe air and communicate with each underwater using whale call sounds.
